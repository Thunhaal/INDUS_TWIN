Possible Values for description:

OVERCONSUMPTION
Machine consuming more power than its expected baseline.

HIGH_VIBRATION
Vibration exceeds the machine's normal operating range.

OVERHEATING
Temperature exceeds the defined safe operating range.

EFFICIENCY_DROP
Power consumption increases without corresponding production increase.

IDLE_OPERATION
Machine remains energized while no useful production activity occurs.

UNPLANNED_DOWNTIME
Unexpected machine stoppage affecting production.

FAULT
Machine enters an abnormal/failure state.

MAINTENANCE
Machine is intentionally taken offline for inspection or repair.
